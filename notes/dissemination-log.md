# Dissemination Log

## Purpose

This log records public dissemination of the fellowship's technical outputs,
channel by channel, with dates and current status. It exists so that
dissemination claims made in fellowship reporting can be checked against a
single maintained record rather than reconstructed from memory.

KPI 3 of the fellowship application targets at least one technical publication
disseminating EU AI standardisation outcomes to the wider developer community,
measured by published URL and analytics.

## Primary Output

**Four ISO Standards, One Missing Layer: Trustworthy AI in Financial Services**

A technical article summarising the repository's cross-standard analysis: what
ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, ISO/IEC 5338, and ISO/IEC TS 25570 each
contribute to trustworthy AI in financial services, and the operational
reassessment layer that sits between them, argued in this repository as
Continuous Reliability Assurance.

- Canonical URL:

- First published: 22 July 2026
- Author: Santhosh Balasa, Yabloko Labs Ltd.
- Funding declared: StandICT.eu 2029 Fellowship

**Reddit r/artificial.** The submission was held by the subreddit's automated

link posts from low-karma accounts. Submission confirmation is retained as
evidence. Visibility should not be assumed until approval is confirmed.

KDnuggets operates editorial selection, so acceptance is not guaranteed and no
publication commitment exists. Recorded here as a submission, not an outcome.

profile, summarising the CRA argument and linking to both the article and this

once. Where a figure is recorded, the capture date must be stated alongside it,
because view counts on all of these platforms continue to move after any
reporting date.

here as they stood, without extrapolation.
